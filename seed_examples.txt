### avoidance
I keep canceling plans and avoiding people, I'd rather be alone.
I don't open my messages, I can't deal with them right now.
I pretend the problem isn't there and distract myself with anything else.
I avoid conflict at all costs, even if it means agreeing to things I don't want.
I don't answer calls, I let them go to voicemail.
I keep changing the subject when it comes up.
I push my feelings down and try not to think about them.
I stay in my comfort zone and avoid anything new or uncertain.
I sleep to avoid dealing with things.
I find any excuse to stay home and not face the situation.
I have 47 unread messages on my phone. Some are from friends, some from work, some from family. I read them in the notification preview and then never open them. It's not that I don't care. It's that opening them means I have to respond. And responding feels impossible right now.
I said yes to dinner three weeks ago. The day arrives and I text them at 4pm saying I don't feel well. I do this to almost every plan I make. I feel relief the moment the text is sent, and then guilt the next morning.
Whenever my partner tries to talk about anything serious — money, the future, how I'm doing — I change the subject. I make a joke. I suddenly need to check something in the other room. I don't know how to stop running from these conversations.
When something upsetting happens I don't cry. I don't talk about it. I don't even let myself think about it. I just push it down and keep going. And then three months later it all comes up at once and I can't understand why I'm falling apart.
I check my phone 200 times a day. Not because I'm waiting for anything. Because being on my phone means I'm not doing anything else. It's the easiest way to avoid everything I should be dealing with.
My friend said something that hurt me and I said nothing. I smiled. I changed the subject. I swallowed it. I'll probably swallow it forever. It's easier than the confrontation I'd have to have, and I'd rather feel bad alone than risk a fight.
There's a stack of envelopes on my counter I haven't opened in three weeks. Some of them are bills. Some of them might be important. I don't know because I won't look. I'm afraid of what's in them.
My phone rings and I let it go to voicemail. Every time. Even when it's someone I love. Even when I want to talk to them. I don't know why I do it. I just can't pick up.
I don't drink to have fun. I drink to not feel. Same with scrolling. Same with sleeping too much. Same with everything I do to avoid being alone with my own thoughts.
I've been putting off a decision for six months. Every time I think about it I find a reason to wait. I ask other people what they think so I don't have to decide. I'm afraid of getting it wrong, so I choose nothing instead.
I keep myself busy so I never have to sit with anything. Work, then gym, then errands, then cooking, then a show, then bed. There's no gap. Because if there's a gap, the feelings show up. And I don't want that.
